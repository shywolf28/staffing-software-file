Recruit CRM publishes three tiers, which sounds simple until you factor in the add-ons that sit outside the base per-seat price — here's what actually determines the right one for a small team.

> **The short answer**
>
> Most agencies under 10 recruiters land on **Pro** (~$85-95/user/month) unless they specifically need SSO, an open API, or sequencing — those require **Business** (~$125-135/user/month).

## The three tiers, as published

- **Pro (~$85-95/user/month annual)** — the entry tier. Covers the core ATS/CRM workflow. Enterprise-level features and open API access are not included.
- **Business (~$125-135/user/month annual)** — adds email sequencing, single sign-on (SSO), audit logs, custom roles, and open API access.
- **Enterprise (~$165-215/user/month annual)** — bundles workflow automation, multiposting, and analytics allowances that are billed separately as add-ons on the lower tiers.

Vendors update pricing periodically, so treat these as a reference point for a sales conversation, not a locked number — confirm current figures directly before budgeting.

## What actually pushes a small team from Pro to Business

The jump usually isn't about headcount — it's about specific features. Watch for these triggers:

- **You need an open API.** If you want to connect Recruit CRM to another tool (a website, a reporting dashboard, a custom integration), that access starts at Business, not Pro.
- **You need SSO.** Relevant mostly for agencies with IT/security requirements from a client or compliance obligation — most small agencies don't need this on day one.
- **You want built-in email sequencing** rather than sending follow-ups manually. This is the feature that most commonly tips a growing small team toward Business.

## The add-ons that apply regardless of tier

Workflow automation, job multiposting, and data enrichment are billed as separate add-ons on Pro and Business — only Enterprise bundles them into the base price. A small agency that wants automation without jumping to Enterprise should budget for these as extra line items, not assume they're included once they've picked a tier.
