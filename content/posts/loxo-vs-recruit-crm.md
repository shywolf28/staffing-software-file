Both show up constantly in "best ATS for agencies" lists, but they're built around different bets on what a small agency needs most: Loxo leans into AI sourcing, Recruit CRM leans into fast, staffing-specific setup.

> **The short answer**
>
> Loxo has a genuinely useful free tier for solo recruiters and stronger built-in sourcing; Recruit CRM is cheaper at team size and faster to get a small team running end to end.

## Pricing, as published

| | Free tier | Entry paid tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Loxo** | Yes — full ATS/CRM, 1 user only | ~$169/user/mo (Basic) | Professional and Enterprise are custom-quoted; AI sourcing/outreach features live above Basic |
| **[Recruit CRM](https://recruitcrm.io?fpr=esteban-fe19e2)** | No | ~$85-95/user/mo (Pro) | Three published tiers, no sales call needed to estimate cost |

Loxo's free tier is real, not a crippled trial — it's the actual ATS/CRM, just capped at one user. That makes it a genuine option for a solo recruiter or a founder testing the software before committing budget, in a way Recruit CRM's entry price doesn't match.

## What each one is actually built for

**Loxo** positions itself as a "Talent Intelligence Platform" — the free and Basic tiers cover core ATS/CRM work, but the AI sourcing database (800M+ profiles per Loxo's own marketing), outreach automation, and AI agents live behind the custom-quoted Professional tier, which buyer reports put in the $250-400/user/month range. If sourcing is your bottleneck rather than pipeline management, that's what you're actually paying for.

**Recruit CRM** doesn't have a comparable sourcing database built in — it's a combined ATS/CRM focused on workflow speed and staffing-specific pipeline management, not candidate discovery. If your agency already has sourcing channels (referrals, job boards, LinkedIn) and needs the software to manage what happens after that, it's the leaner, cheaper fit.

## Which one actually fits

Solo recruiter or pre-revenue agency testing the waters: Loxo's free tier costs nothing and is a real ATS, not a stripped demo. Small team (2-5 recruiters) that needs sourcing built in: Loxo Basic, accepting the higher per-seat cost. Small team that already sources candidates elsewhere and wants fast, affordable pipeline management: Recruit CRM.

<div class="label">Bottom line</div>
<p>Loxo wins if sourcing is the bottleneck and you're solo or willing to pay more per seat. Recruit CRM wins on cost and speed once you already have a sourcing channel.</p>
