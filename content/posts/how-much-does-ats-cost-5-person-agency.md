Most ATS pricing pages quote a per-seat number, which makes the real monthly bill for a small team easy to underestimate until you multiply it out.

- **Loxo and JobAdder** don't publish consistent per-seat pricing — budget for a sales quote rather than a self-serve estimate, and treat any number you find online as approximate until confirmed.

These are subscription costs only. Vendors update pricing periodically, so treat these as a starting point for comparison shopping, not a locked quote — always confirm current numbers directly before budgeting.

## What the sticker price doesn't include

- **Add-ons billed separately.** Recruit CRM, for example, bills workflow automation, job multiposting, and data enrichment as add-ons on top of the base per-seat price — easy to miss when comparing base tiers across vendors.
- **Annual contract requirements.** Some platforms, Bullhorn included, are typically sold on annual terms rather than month-to-month, which changes your effective commitment even if the per-month number looks similar.
- **Implementation and migration.** Ask directly whether historical candidate/client data migration is included in the subscription or billed as a separate service — this varies a lot by vendor and can add real one-time cost.

## The actual decision

At 5 seats, the gap between Recruit CRM's Pro tier and Bullhorn's Core tier is roughly $400/month, or close to $5,000/year — a meaningful number for a small agency. That gap is usually the price of Bullhorn's larger partner ecosystem and infrastructure built for bigger operations, which is worth paying for if you're confident you'll grow past 10-15 recruiters soon, and less worth paying for if you're not.

Before comparing sticker prices further, get a real quote from each vendor for your exact seat count — published tier pricing is a starting point, and vendors will sometimes negotiate on annual commitments that public pricing pages don't reflect.
