"Integrates with LinkedIn" gets used loosely in ATS marketing pages, and it covers four genuinely different things — knowing which one you actually need before you shop saves a lot of confused sales calls.

> **The short answer**
>
> If you're paying for a LinkedIn Recruiter seat, look specifically for **Recruiter System Connect (RSC)** support — that's the real two-way sync. The other "LinkedIn integrations" vendors advertise are usually something lighter.

## The four types of LinkedIn integration, and why they're not the same

- **LinkedIn Job Slots** — paid premium job placement on LinkedIn. This is a LinkedIn product, not really an ATS integration, and the cost is separate from your ATS bill entirely.
- **LinkedIn Easy Apply** — candidates apply using their LinkedIn profile data, which flows into your ATS. The most common and lightest-weight integration; most modern ATS platforms support this.
- **Recruiter System Connect (RSC)** — the real two-way sync. InMail history, recruiter notes, pipeline status, and profile activity stay synchronized between LinkedIn Recruiter and your ATS automatically, so a stage change in one system reflects in the other without manual copying.
- **Profile import** — a lighter-weight, one-directional pull of LinkedIn profile data into a candidate record, without the bidirectional sync RSC provides.

## The part that catches agencies off guard

RSC only does anything if you already have a **LinkedIn Recruiter license** — a separate LinkedIn product, not something your ATS provides. Buyer-reported pricing on that license runs roughly $9,000-15,000 per seat per year as of 2026, sometimes bundled differently depending on the LinkedIn edition. If your agency doesn't have Recruiter seats, an ATS's RSC support is irrelevant to you — you'd be evaluating a feature you can't use yet.

So the actual question to answer first isn't "does this ATS integrate with LinkedIn" — it's "do we have, or are we about to buy, LinkedIn Recruiter seats." If yes, RSC support matters and is worth confirming directly with each ATS vendor, since not every platform supports it. If no, Easy Apply support (which is far more common and doesn't require a Recruiter license) is the integration that actually affects your day-to-day.

## What to ask a vendor directly

- "Do you support Recruiter System Connect, or just Easy Apply / profile import?"
- "Is RSC included, or a paid add-on?"
- "What data actually syncs both ways — notes, stage, InMail history, or just contact info?"
