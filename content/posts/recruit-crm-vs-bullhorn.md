These are the two names that come up most when a small staffing agency starts shopping for an ATS, and they represent genuinely different bets on where your agency is headed.

> **The short answer**
>
> Recruit CRM wins on transparent pricing and fast setup for teams under five recruiters. Bullhorn wins on ecosystem depth and infrastructure maturity if you're confident you'll scale past ten within a year or two.

## What each one is actually built for

**Recruit CRM** is purpose-built for recruiting and staffing agencies specifically, and it shows in setup time — it's one of the more frequently cited tools for getting a small team productive within a day rather than a multi-week rollout. It holds a strong reputation among smaller agencies on review sites, and its pricing structure (three published tiers, clear add-on costs) makes it easier to estimate your real monthly bill before you talk to sales.

**Bullhorn** is the more established, enterprise-oriented platform — used by a large base of agencies including sizable enterprise accounts. That scale brings a mature partner marketplace (300+ integrations at the Core tier) and infrastructure built for agencies that plan to grow well past ten recruiters. The trade-off is that Bullhorn's own Starter tier is explicitly a small-team on-ramp: at 1-2 users, you're expected to graduate to Core reasonably quickly as you add headcount, and pricing beyond that increasingly goes through custom quotes rather than a public rate card.

## Which one actually fits

If you're under five recruiters and want to know your monthly cost before a sales call, Recruit CRM's transparent tiered pricing and staffing-specific focus make it easier to evaluate on your own. If you're already planning to scale past ten recruiters within the next year or two, or you want the deeper partner ecosystem and infrastructure maturity of a platform built for larger operations, Bullhorn is worth the extra setup overhead and less predictable pricing.

Neither is a bad choice — this is really a question of how much you value pricing transparency and fast setup right now versus long-runway scalability.
